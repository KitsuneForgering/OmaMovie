# CapCut: what people like (and dislike)

> Research done on 2026-10-02. Sourced facts are linked in the [Sources](#sources) section.
> Part of the CapCut sources are promotional material or written by competitors (e.g. VEED,
> Podcastle). Read prices and complaints with that in mind.

---

## 1. Summary

CapCut (ByteDance) is the most popular editor among short-form creators. People like it for
**time to result**:

1. **Templates** where you only swap the clips;
2. editable **automatic captions**;
3. ready-made **"trending" effects** (speed ramps, transitions, stickers);
4. a **clean**, drag-and-drop **interface** you learn in minutes;
5. **cross-platform** with sync (phone → desktop).

And dislike: an **aggressive paywall** (the Pro price doubled in 2025), **terms of service**
that give ByteDance a perpetual license over content (including drafts), **political
instability** (bans) and **crashes that lose work**.

For OmaMovie, CapCut shows **which high-level operations the audience wants**, and its weak
points are obvious differentiators: offline, no account, no paywall and the user's content
under the user's control.

## 2. What people like

### 2.1 Time to result
The most repeated argument: going from raw footage to a finished video in minutes. Templates,
presets and automation exist for that.

### 2.2 Templates
- Ready templates where the user **only swaps photos or clips**.
- Templates follow **trends** (TikTok integration), making it easy to join trends and viral challenges.

### 2.3 Automatic captions
- Generated by AI from the audio and **editable** afterwards.
- Important because a lot of social video is watched **without sound**.
- The feature most often cited as "saves a lot of time".

### 2.4 Speed ramps (speed curves)
- Speed > Curve with named presets: **bullet, montage, jump cut, hero time, flash in, flash
  out**, all customizable.
- They create progressive slow motion and accelerations without manual keyframes.

### 2.5 Other high-level features
| Feature | What it does |
|---|---|
| Keyframes | On "every setting" |
| Auto Reframe | Detects the subject and recomposes for another aspect ratio (e.g. horizontal → vertical) |
| Beat Sync | Aligns cuts to the music's beat |
| Text-to-speech | Several voices and languages |
| Background removal | Without a green screen |
| Chroma key, stabilization, motion tracking | Free when CapCut became popular |
| Library of stickers, effects, transitions, audio | Ready-made content |

### 2.6 Interface
- "Clean, straightforward", drag and drop; you start editing within minutes.
- A conventional multitrack timeline on desktop.
- **Mobile-first**: the phone version is the most polished; the desktop one is described as less polished.
- iOS, Android, web and desktop: start on the phone and finish on the computer.

## 3. What people dislike

| Problem | Detail |
|---|---|
| **Price** | In May 2025 Pro went from US$ 9.99 to US$ 19.99/month. Dynamic captions, effects and cloud storage moved behind the paywall |
| **Billing and support** | Complaints about charges after cancellation and slow support, limited to FAQs and email |
| **Terms of service (June 2025)** | A **perpetual, irrevocable, worldwide, royalty-free** license for ByteDance over all uploaded content, **including unpublished drafts**, even after deleting the account. A risk for agencies and NDA-bound content |
| **Availability** | Banned in India since 2020; in the US, banned in January 2025 and, according to VEED's review, unavailable for new downloads in US app stores |
| **Content removal** | May remove content without notice |
| **Stability** | Reports of crashes while editing with **lost work** |

## 4. Implications for OmaMovie

### 4.1 Obvious differentiators

What CapCut does not offer and OmaMovie offers by definition:

- **Offline and local**: media and project never leave the machine.
- **No account, no terms over the user's content, no feature paywall, no watermark.**
- **Stability and preservation of work**: atomic save and autosave (`CLAUDE.md` §14). Lost work
  is the most serious complaint about any editor.

### 4.2 Features to consider and their architectural impact

| CapCut feature | Recommendation | Architectural impact |
|---|---|---|
| **Speed ramps with presets** | Yes, medium term | **Mapping timeline time → media time cannot be "constant speed" only.** It needs a **curve** (piecewise monotonic function), evaluated in `RationalTime`. Decide in ADR-0002 (time representation) **before** implementing speed, even if the first UI only has constant speed. Frame interpolation (optical flow) is a later, separate step |
| **Keyframes everywhere** | Yes | Already planned. Effect and transform parameters must be animatable by default, not by exception |
| **Captions as a first-class element** | Yes | A **caption/text** track type in the model, with SRT/VTT import/export. Generating captions automatically needs transcription, an AI non-goal; if it ever lands, prefer a **local** (offline) model and keep the result as ordinary editable captions |
| **Changing the project aspect ratio (vertical/horizontal)** | Yes | The project canvas must be resizable without breaking the edit. Decide in the compositor ADR whether transforms are stored in **normalized coordinates** or pixels, and what happens when the aspect ratio changes. Auto Reframe (subject detection) comes later |
| **Templates** | Future | Same conclusion as iMovie: a template produces a normal, editable timeline (`imovie.md` §7) |
| **Beat sync** | Future | Needs beat detection (audio analysis, no heavy AI) and timeline markers. Markers must exist in the model |
| **Named effect/transition presets** | Yes | Presets are data (saved parameters) over generic effects, like iMovie's overlay presets |
| **Text-to-speech, background removal, stock** | Not now | Non-goals (AI/cloud) |
| **Cross-platform sync** | No | Non-goal (cloud). A file-based project already lets users sync with their own tools |

### 4.3 Audience

CapCut shows that the vertical short-form audience is large and wants **outcome operations**
("make it dramatic slow motion"), not **mechanism operations** ("add a speed keyframe"). For
OmaMovie: the inspector offers the intent preset first and the parameters afterwards. The same
principle as iMovie's named operations.

### 4.4 What not to copy

- A "trends" library and stock content (needs a server).
- A mobile-first interface.
- Account and cloud dependence.
