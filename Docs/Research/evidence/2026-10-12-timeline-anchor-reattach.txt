Timeline: edit cost with many connected clips (2026-10-12)

Bench (scratch, not versioned): tests/timeline/fixture.hpp's 30 fps fixture, 5,000 storyline
clips of 5 frames, the sound of every second clip detached (connected to its picture since
ADR-0014's 2026-10-12 note), then 20 splits near the end; mean per split, release libs.

Every edit on a timeline with connected clips runs detail::reattach, which looked up each
connected clip's primary with Timeline::find_clip / track_of: a scan of every track per
connected clip, O(connected × clips). It now builds one ID index per reattach.

  connected   before      after
        0     4.27 ms     3.94 ms
      500     6.27 ms     6.65 ms
    2,000    19.72 ms    14.05 ms

The quadratic term is gone but most of the cost remains: the before/after place maps, the
pass over every clip and the exact source arithmetic per connected clip are linear and were
not profiled further (no perf on this machine). Both stay under the long-form gate's p95 of
100 ms; revisit with a profile if connected clips grow past a few thousand.
