Picture fidelity: PSNR of the export against its source (ffmpeg psnr filter, all 300 frames)
  source tagged BT.709 (scale=out_color_matrix=bt709, -colorspace bt709, crf 12):
      Y 36.3 dB, U 35.5, V 33.8, average 35.7 (libx264 default crf 23 on a busy test pattern)
  untagged source encoded by ffmpeg with BT.601: 25.8 dB average, with the hue shift of a
      BT.601/BT.709 mismatch: OmaMovie reads untagged HD as BT.709 (the common convention),
      ffmpeg had encoded BT.601. A policy question for untagged input, not an export defect.

A/V offset: 1280x720 29.97 fps source with a one-frame white flash and a 1 kHz beep at 2 s and
5 s (beep onsets quantized by lavfi to 2.0201 s and 5.0156 s). Flash frames found with
signalstats, beep onsets as the first sample above 0.05.
  before  export beeps 23.2 ms early at both marks, flashes unchanged: one AAC frame at 44.1 kHz.
          Cause: AudioDecoder::seek started output after the target (the first packet after a
          seek decodes short: AAC priming/MDCT overlap); the mixer trusted the seek's promise and
          placed that audio at the target. The same mixer drives playback.
  after   seek demuxes 200 ms early and trims to the target: export beeps at 2.0201 s and
          5.0156 s, equal to the source; flashes at 2.002 s and 5.005 s in both.
          Regression tests: AAC seeks are sample-exact; Opus in Matroska still lands 24 samples
          (0.5 ms) late after a seek (open).

Not measured: long exports (two hours), hardware encoders, export while playing back.
