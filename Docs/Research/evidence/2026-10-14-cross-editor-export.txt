editors (sizes and PSNR below), so these are separate profiles, not one quality target.

hung: the viewer composited one GPU frame on two layers and the render thread deadlocked on the
frame lock it already held (core analysed; Docs/implementation-plan-code-review-4.md).
OmaMovie spends ~2 s of system time per export against melt's ~0.4-0.9 s; not yet explained.
