M6 Recordings refresh (2026-10-12)

Machine: Intel Iris Xe laptop, ext4 SSD, warm page cache, g++ -O2.
Bench: a scratch program calls omarchy::list_recordings(dir, 12) 30 times on synthetic
folders of N files ("screenrecording-i.mp4", every tenth a "-preview.png"). This is the
directory scan that Session::refreshRecordings runs on the UI thread when Projects opens
and when the folder changes; the rest of the refresh touches only the 12 shown entries.
No capture was in progress (/tmp/omarchy-screenrecord-filename absent).

Before (two stats per video file: mtime and an unused size; full sort; when a capture is
in progress, std::filesystem::equivalent added two more stats per file):
     20 files: p50  0.047  p95  0.075  max  0.078 ms
  1,000 files: p50  3.136  p95  4.640  max 16.485 ms
 10,000 files: p50 33.390  p95 52.038  max 69.747 ms

After (one stat per video file; a heap of the newest 12, O(files log 12), entries older
than the oldest kept are not copied; equivalent() only for files changed in the last 5 s):
     20 files: p50  0.029  p95  0.052  max  0.101 ms
  1,000 files: p50  1.222  p95  1.550  max  1.638 ms
 10,000 files: p50 14.848  p95 15.367  max 15.403 ms

Decision: below one 60 Hz frame even at 10,000 files, so enumeration stays on the UI
thread. Not measured: a cold cache or network filesystem, and the in-progress capture case
(it would require writing the capture script's shared /tmp file).
