Timeline: inserting clips into a long track (2026-10-12)

Bench (scratch, not versioned): tests/timeline/fixture.hpp's 30 fps fixture; N edit::append
steps of 5 frames each executed as one transaction, against the release libs (g++ -O2).
Found while the M6 long-timeline audit spent 1.7-2.2 s building 5,000 clips.

Cause: InsertClip::apply pushed the clip at the end and re-sorted the whole track
(O(n log n) moves of large Clip values per insert), so building n clips cost O(n² log n).
Tracks are sorted after every primitive command, so the clip now goes in at
upper_bound(start) instead.

              before        after
   1,000      44.4 ms        2.5 ms
   5,000   1,247.8 ms       34.3 ms
  20,000  29,486.3 ms      993.5 ms

Still O(n) per insert: the duplicate-ID check (Timeline::find_clip) and range_free scan the
track. At the 5,000-clip long-form gate that is 34 ms for the whole build, so they stay.
timeline_tests (423 checks) pass, also under ASan/UBSan.
