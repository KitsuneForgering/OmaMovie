M10 sustained workload, bounded headless pass (2026-10-14)

tests/app/test_sustained.cpp: each cycle opens a Session on the 1 s camera fixture, splits,
plays 150 ms, saves the project, exports it in software (320x180, 30 frames) and destroys the
Session. Descriptors (/proc/self/fd) and VmRSS are read after each cycle.

Descriptors constant; resident memory settles near 150 MB after about nine cycles (allocator,
Mesa and FFmpeg pools warming), then wanders within ~3 MB: no growth per cycle.

Default 8 cycles in the suite (fails on any descriptor growth after two warm-up cycles, or
64 MiB of resident growth): debug 3.9 s, TSan 5 s (379 -> 424 MiB, tapering), ASan 7.8 s
(resident bound skipped: quarantine grows ~40 MiB per cycle by design; LSan checks leaks).

Not covered: the long-form gate (hours of media, interaction latency percentiles, VRAM, cache
and disk use) and a committed per-machine baseline.
