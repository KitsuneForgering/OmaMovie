# M11 inventory: mixed ownership and change coupling (2026-10-14)

First M11 item: which selected paths mix responsibilities, change together or are hard to test.
Measured at commit 47b7b1a; sizes in lines (`wc -l`). Size only locates candidates.

Twelve `session_*.cpp` units (29–80 lines) already hold small Session features.

## Findings

1. **`session.cpp` section labels no longer match their contents.** "project files (ADR-0007)"
   (810 lines) holds about 600 lines of clip video adjustments: LUT, colour, effects, transitions,
   framing, opacity/volume/transform keys, Ken Burns. "captions" (447 lines) holds the source
   viewer and the whole transport (`togglePlay`, `shuttle`, `setSpeed`, `startPlayback`, `pause`,
   `onTick`, `adaptPreview`). A reader cannot find either by the headings.
2. **Transport state is concentrated but unowned.** `speed_`, `tick_*`, `scheduler_` and `audio_`
   are read or written almost only by `startPlayback` (9 uses), `onTick` (8), `pause` (5),
   `shuttle`, `setSpeed` and `adaptPreview`, plus one-line accessors. That is a boundary in all
   but name: a `Playback` owner held by Session could be driven and checked without a project.
3. **Change coupling.** Of 43 commits touching `session.cpp` since 2026-10-01, 31 also touch
   `session.hpp`, 17 `main.cpp` (now mostly the smoke), 15 `Main.qml` and 10 `TimelineView.qml`:
   a feature usually lands as Session method + header + QML wiring + smoke step. 28 of the 43 are
   `feat(ui)`.
4. **Testability.** Video adjustments and edits are already reachable through Session's public
   API from `tests/app` (no window). The transport is not: its tests need a Session, a project
   and real time (`test_session_stress`, `test_source_playback`).
5. **QML.** Lane clips now share `LaneDrag`; the storyline clip's MouseArea still repeats its press,
   slip/slide and move logic. `Main.qml` holds the action table, dialogs and layout state together.

## Candidates, ranked

1. **Extract the transport into an explicit owner** (finding 2). Real ownership gain and a
   testable clock, but it moves `AudioPlayer` and `VideoScheduler` ownership and their threads:
   per CLAUDE.md §13/§21 its lifetime and threading impact must be written down first, and the
   threading model is an ask-before change.
2. **Fix the section labels or split `session.cpp` along findings 1** (video adjustments,
   source viewer, transport). Cheap and safe, but it only aids navigation; M11 does not accept
   it as a completion by itself.
3. **Share the storyline clip gesture with `LaneDrag`** (finding 5), keeping its magnetic reorder.
4. Not proposed: a service layer over Session, or splitting by file size alone.

Baseline (the second item) is still to record: interaction latency percentiles, playback drops
and memory on the M6/M10 fixtures, and screenshots at wide, half-width and 640 px.
